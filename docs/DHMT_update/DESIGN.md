## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance visual language bridging corporate sophistication with the disciplined energy of competitive athletics. Tailored for corporate multi-sport tournaments, executive athletic retreats, and enterprise fitness initiatives, the experience delivers precision, clarity, and architectural restraint. 

The aesthetic is Modern Minimalist Flat. It discards sensory clutter, saturated rainbow cards, and gratuitous gradients in favor of structured geometry, expansive breathing room, razor-sharp hairline borders, and pure tonal contrast. The interface projects prestige, athletic excellence, and unwavering institutional trust.

## Colors

The palette is strictly controlled, anchored by a deep slate navy core, clinical white canvas zones, and subtle cool slates, punctuated only by an athletic royal blue to direct optical focus.

- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Navy Slate. Serves as the primary brand ink, dominant headings, primary action fills, and high-impact corporate anchor sections.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: Royal Electric Blue. Used sparingly for interactive focal points, active state highlights, live scores, and primary callout accents.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`)**: Refined Cerulean Teal. Reserved for secondary telemetry metrics, tournament stats, and operational indicators.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate Gray. Drives metadata, auxiliary labels, secondary text tiers, and disabled contexts.

### Supporting Tonal Tokens
- **Canvas Base**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Surface Muted**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for section alternations and card backgrounds
- **Surface Hover**: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) for interactive control surfaces
- **Border Subtle**: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for structural hairlines
- **Border Strong**: `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) for active form states and key dividers
- **Inverse Surface**: `#0F172A` with text `#F8FAFC`

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Work Sans** across all roles to ensure geometric balance, high legibility across dense athletic data tables, and an executive corporate tone.

- **Display & Headlines**: Tightly kerned with negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to impart an editorial, sports-journalism gravity without visual excess.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for maximum scanning speed, featuring generous line heights (1.5x - 1.6x) and neutral slate-to-navy pigmentation.
- **Labels & Micro-Data**: Configured with uppercase transformations and wider letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) for sports brackets, team divisional tags, timers, and corporate classifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt structural spatial cadence organized within a 12-column responsive fluid grid.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Dynamic stack behavior for match cards and tournament fixtures.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8 columns, `margin-md: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, `margin-lg: 5rem`, `gutter-lg: 2rem`, with a maximum container threshold of `1280px`.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Large breathing sections (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem-6rem in section wrappers) establish institutional calm, counteracting the high density of sports tournament schedules and corporate rosters.

## Elevation & Depth

This system operates on a zero-to-negligible shadow philosophy. Depth is communicated strictly through surface layering, micro-contrast, and hairline borders.

- **Zero Heavy Shadows**: Avoid blurred, ambient, or multi-stop drop shadows. Drop shadows are set to `none`.
- **Hairline Bounding (1px Borders)**: Structural bounding boxes use a crisp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). In active or focused states, borders step up to `#0F172A` or `#2563EB`.
- **Tonal Stepping**: 
  - Base document layer: `#FFFFFF`
  - Inset containers / cards: `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` border, or a pure `#F8FAFC` fill without border.
  - Interactive hover state: Smooth transition to `#F1F5F9` background or a 1px border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Precision Floating Elevation**: Modal overlays and floating contextual menus alone carry a single architectural shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, framed with a 1px hairline border in `#E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft** geometry (`roundedness: 1`), relying on precise, low-radius curvature (`4px` base, `8px` for larger modular cards). 

- **Containers, Cards, Inputs, Buttons**: Fixed to `4px` (`0.25rem`). This preserves crisp, athletic lines and corporate discipline.
- **Pill Shapes (`9999px`)**: Exclusively reserved for status badges, live event indicators, match minute tags, and divisional category chips to signal athletic telemetry and metadata.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `none`, `4px` radius. Hover: `#1E293B`. Active: `#020617`.
- **Secondary / Action Accent**: Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `none`. Hover: `#1D4ED8`.
- **Outline / Flat**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Dimensions**: Small (32px height, 12px horizontal padding), Medium (40px height, 16px horizontal padding), Large (48px height, 24px horizontal padding).

### Cards & Match Panels
- **Container**: Flat `#FFFFFF` fill, bounded by `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `4px` border radius, zero shadow.
- **Section Headers within Cards**: Separated by a `1px solid #F1F5F9` bottom divider, paired with uppercase `label-md` headers in `#64748B`.
- **Hover Behavior**: No vertical lift or movement. Border color shifts from `#E2E8F0` to `#CBD5E1`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Format**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `24px`, padding `0 10px`.
- **Live / Active Match**: Background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)`, text `#2563EB`, hairline border `1px solid rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`.
- **Neutral / Divisional**: Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Form Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Height `40px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `4px` radius, text `#0F172A`. Focused state: border `1px solid #0F172A` with an outer ring of `2px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: `16px x 16px`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked state: background `#0F172A`, border `#0F172A`, inner icon pure white.

### Lists & Tournament Brackets
- **List Rows**: Horizontal rule division using `1px solid #F1F5F9`. Alternating zebra striping is prohibited; subtle hover shift to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Bracket Connector Lines**: Clean `1px solid #CBD5E1` vectors without rounded corners, maintaining technical precision across fixtures.

### Telemetry & Score Counters
- Displayed with tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) in `#0F172A` with bold weights, flanked by uppercase sub-labels (`label-sm`) in `#64748B`.
